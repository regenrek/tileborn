# AI Specification

## Model Boundaries

No model runs in the product feature. AI agents may implement and review Planr
items but cannot weaken ownership, proof, security, or cleanup requirements.

## Prompt Contracts

Workers lease only items from this plan, preserve the recorded ownership split,
and must not introduce PartyKit dependencies or adjacent Alchemy/content work.

## Evaluation

Evaluation is executable transport and UI evidence, not model judgment.
